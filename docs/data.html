What every column means

Every scored answer, one row each: three wordings × three constants for E1, 40 strings for E3, 30 for E4b, 8 planted digits for E5 and π, e and √2 for E6. Error positions use S for a swapped digit, I for an inserted one and D for a dropped one, followed by the position in the expected text. The model's own words aren't published here, only their scores.

Teacher forcing on the E2 π text. Each row is one version, and each column one digit position: position n is the digit that follows n correct ones, so a free recitation that stops at 813 went wrong at position 813. Hover over a cell for its numbers, and click one to list the positions around it. How teacher forcing works.
